Wiring Lab

Pin-accurate connections from the firmware netlist. Load the reference design, validate electrical rules (including “don’t put 5V on the mic”), then simulate a talk turn before you cut any wires.

Pin map — one wire at a time

Drawing every net at once is unreadable. Pick a connection from the list (or click a pin). Only that wire is drawn, with labeled endpoints. Clicking a pin narrows the list so you can trace one matching wire at a time.

    Build & validate

    This is not SPICE — it checks the SomethingFishy pin map and catch-you-later mistakes (5V on mic VDD, motors on raw GPIOs, missing ENA/ENB jumpers). A passing sim means the firmware’s talk-turn path has the nets it needs.

    After a PASS

    Follow the bypass guide, wire the real hardware, then flash firmware with your Wi‑Fi and OpenAI key.
